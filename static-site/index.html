08 — Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping foot inside Presson Fitness Gym.

Are beginners welcome at Presson Fitness Gym?

Yes, absolutely! While our gym has serious equipment for powerlifters and strength athletes, over 50% of our members started as complete beginners. Our on-duty coaches are always on the floor to teach proper form, spot your sets, and keep you safe.

Do I need to book in advance before working out?

For open gym access and general workouts, no reservation is needed — you can walk in anytime during our operating hours (8:00 AM – 10:00 PM). For 1-on-1 personal training assessments, please reserve your preferred time via Form B above or by messaging our coach.

What should I bring on my first visit?

Just bring clean training shoes (flat soles or cross-trainers recommended), comfortable workout clothes, and a valid photo ID. We provide free drinking water, clean showers, and secure lockers.

Is there parking available on-site?

Yes, we offer free parking for cars and motorcycles directly in front of the gym along Fitness Avenue.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer (BDO, BPI), and major Credit/Debit cards at our front desk.
